# Example Topics — Tone & Pacing Reference

Use these as the tone/rhythm bar for any new scene copy. Each follows:
hook → plain fact → deeper/surprising fact or myth-bust → reflective close.

## Example 1 — The Mary Rose (maritime disaster)

- title: "The Ship That Sank in Front of the King"
- topic: Maritime Disaster
- hookDate: "July 19" / hookYear: "1545"
- scene2Headline: "Henry VIII's Proudest Warship"
- scene2Subline: "Built to rule the English Channel"
- scene3Headline: "One Gust of Wind"
- scene3Body: "Seawater poured through her open gunports and she went down in minutes — right in front of the king."
- scene4Headline: "Trapped by Her Own Nets"
- scene4Body: "Anti-boarding nets meant to protect the crew instead trapped them below deck. Over 400 men drowned."
- scene5Headline: "Found After 437 Years"
- scene5Body: "Her wreck was rediscovered and raised in the 1980s, astonishingly preserved — now on display in Portsmouth."
- scene6Cta: "Follow for daily maritime history."

## Example 2 — The Vasa (maritime disaster)

- title: "The Vasa: Sunk in 20 Minutes"
- topic: Maritime Disaster
- hookDate: "August 10" / hookYear: "1628"
- scene2Headline: "Sweden's Mightiest Warship"
- scene2Subline: "Built to rule the Baltic Sea"
- scene3Headline: "A Fatal Design Flaw"
- scene3Body: "Top-heavy with two full gun decks, the Vasa was unstable before she even left the harbor."
- scene4Headline: "Down in Minutes"
- scene4Body: "A single gust of wind tipped her over less than a mile into her maiden voyage. 30 people drowned."
- scene5Headline: "Found After 333 Years"
- scene5Body: "Recovered almost perfectly intact in 1961 — she's now the centerpiece of a Stockholm museum."
- scene6Cta: "Follow for daily maritime history."

## Non-maritime example — for general "on this day" topics

- title: "The Fire That Burned Rome"
- topic: Disaster / Myth-bust
- hookDate: "July 19" / hookYear: "64"
- scene2Headline: "Rome Was Burning"
- scene2Subline: "A fire that lasted six days"
- scene3Headline: "The Emperor Got Blamed"
- scene3Body: "Legend says Nero fiddled while Rome burned — a story that stuck for centuries."
- scene4Headline: "He Wasn't Even There"
- scene4Body: "Historical records place Nero miles away when the fire started. He returned to help organize relief."
- scene5Headline: "A Myth That Outlived the Truth"
- scene5Body: "Some legends are more memorable than the facts — this one still shows up in movies and textbooks today."
- scene6Cta: "Follow for daily history."

## Writing rules

- Scene 4 is the emotional/dramatic peak — always the strongest line in the reel
- Keep each scene body to 1–2 short sentences (fits ~4–5 second read time)
- Prefer concrete numbers/details (dates, counts, durations) over vague language
- End Scene 5 on a reflective or "why it still matters" note, not just a fact dump
